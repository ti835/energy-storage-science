## Abstract

An unknown quantum operation, like an unknown quantum state, cannot be perfectly copied or stored for later use. A specific gate can be embedded in an entangled resource and teleported, but its destination is fixed when the resource is created. Here we show that the capability to apply a two-qudit entangling gate can instead be secret-shared among many parties: the shares are distributed in advance, and a later key release lets exactly one of them, chosen after distribution, exercise it.
