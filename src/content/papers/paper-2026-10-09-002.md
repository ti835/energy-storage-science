## Abstract

The LZ 248 keV recoil candidate admits an interpretation in terms of pseudo-Dirac dark matter with a mixing-suppressed inelastic $Z$ interaction. In the MSSM, the Higgsino limit conflicts with the adopted solar-neutrino bound, while a gaugino realization requires an unprotected mass degeneracy. We realize such spectra in the MRSSM, where an $R$-symmetric Dirac bino produces the required $Z$ transition and wino coannihilation supplies the observed relic abundance.
