## Abstract

We construct $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Yang-Mills-Higgs fields on a broad class of noncompact Riemannian manifolds, concentrating in the large-mass limit along prescribed nondegenerate minimal submanifolds of codimension three. The solutions have uniformly bounded rescaled energy. On closed manifolds with $b_1(M)=0$, adding a suitably scaled Higgs potential gives an analogous concentration result.
