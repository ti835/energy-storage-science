## Abstract

Equilibrium quantum many-body methods differ not only in approximation, but in which information they represent explicitly. We formulate this distinction by fixing an equilibrium specification and viewing every representation as an encoder from admissible states to represented variables. The identity encoder gives a full-state representation, whereas a non-injective encoder gives a reduced representation whose value labels a fiber of compatible states.
