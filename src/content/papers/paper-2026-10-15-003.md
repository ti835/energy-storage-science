## Abstract

We study quantum lifting of classical BPS cohomology classes in four-dimensional $\mathcal N=4$ super-Yang-Mills theory. Certain powers of the gaugino bilinear $\mathcal S=\text{Tr}(λ_+λ_-)$ in orthogonal theories provide non-Coulomb monotones: multi-graviton classes that vanish upon Cartan restriction.
