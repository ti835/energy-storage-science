## Abstract

Giant pulses are rare bright radio bursts that occur in restricted ranges of pulsar rotational phase. Here we report giant pulses from PSR B0355+54, a pulsar with spin period ~0.156 s. Using 7.97 hours of observations centred at 1.46 GHz, with 128 MHz bandwidth in each circular polarizations, we identify 432 pulse periods containing bright pulses.
