## Abstract

We study the differential Poisson sigma model (DPSM) in the symplectic case and show that its classical reduction defines a distinguished class of A-type models on symplectic targets, not necessarily Kähler. The DPSM is a covariant first-order sigma model whose graded target is the parity-shifted tangent bundle $T[1]M$ of a Poisson manifold $M$.
