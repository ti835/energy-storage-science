## Abstract

We show that Warm Little Inflation is anomalous, lacks a suitable inflaton potential, and cannot sustain the thermal bath, as the quasi-static approximation over-estimated the dissipated power. To address these issues, we propose a new friction mechanism and new anomaly-free models based on one fermion charged under a U(1) gauge group in one extra dimension.
