## Abstract

Infinite Energy (by Light Potato, 2025) is a single-player puzzle in which one wires a network of sockets so that, after every power source is unplugged, energy keeps circulating and leaves every board energized. We study several variants of the game and the complexity of deciding whether an instance can be won. The variants can be played at https://infinite-energy-peer-review.netlify.app, which also has worked examples of the reduction techniques.
