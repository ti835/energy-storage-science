## Abstract

Recent cosmological observations have renewed interest in non-standard late-time evolution. We investigate whether departures from the standard expansion history are accompanied by modifications of gravitational dynamics through a joint, non-parametric reconstruction of the effective dark energy density, $Ω_{\rm X}(a)$, and the gravitational response functions $μ(a)$ and $Σ(a)$.
