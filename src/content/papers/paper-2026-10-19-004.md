## Abstract

Spin triplet superconductors are of interest because of their topological properties, and their robustness to high magnetic fields. CeRh$_2$As$_2$ is a unique heavy fermion superconductor with both even-parity and candidate odd-parity superconducting phases with a special feature enabling a field induced phase transition between the two. The field induced transition which switches the parity of the Cooper pairs is enabled by a staggered antisymmetric spin orbit coupling (ASOC) interaction. Howev
