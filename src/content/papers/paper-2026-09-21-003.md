## Abstract

In classical systems, rugged potential energy landscapes provide a transparent mechanism for the celebrated Mpemba effect, in which hotter initial states cool down faster. This picture generally does not survive in quantum systems. Here we show how to design a quantum energy landscape with local dissipation leading to an Mpemba effect with parametrically separated timescales. Our approach uses Stark localization to design an energy landscape and localized incoherent hopping as cooling mechanism.
