## Abstract

We prove optimal non-uniqueness of Leray solutions of the unforced incompressible Navier-Stokes equations in 3D within the class of axially symmetric swirl-free flows. More precisely, we construct two solutions $u_1,u_2$ that have the same initial data $u_1(0)=u_2(0)$, are smooth for every positive time, satisfy the energy identity, and satisfy \[ u_1,u_2\in C_tH_x^α\cap L_t^2H_x^{α+1}\,\,\text{ for every }\,\,0\leqα&lt;1/2,\qquad u_1,u_2\in L_t^\infty L_x^{3,\infty}\cap L_t^{2,\infty}L_x^\infty
