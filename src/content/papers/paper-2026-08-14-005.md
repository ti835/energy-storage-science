## Abstract

We propose an unconventional microscopic origin for the fractional quantum anomalous Hall (FQAH) effect in rhombohedral graphene moiré superlattices: skyrmion fractionalization. We view the state at filling $ν&lt;1$ as a metal of skyrmion vacancies, charge $+e$ objects formed by removing layer-pseudospin skyrmions from the interaction-generated skyrmion lattice Chern insulator at $ν=1$. These vacancies are intrinsically multiband degrees of freedom, absent in single Chern band-projected studies.
