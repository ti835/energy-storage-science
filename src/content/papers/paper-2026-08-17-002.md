## Abstract

A fundamental open question in hadron spectroscopy is whether the radial excitation ladder of quarkonia truncates at a finite level---a possibility that would challenge the conventional quark-antiquark bound-state picture and offer decisive clues to the nonperturbative strong interaction. Taking advantage of the newly observed high-mass hadronic states, we address this issue by solving a screened Godfrey--Isgur Hamiltonian for charmonium and bottomonium using the Gaussian expansion method.
