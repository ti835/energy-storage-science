## Abstract

Video Large Language Models (Video-LLMs) have made rapid progress on temporal video understanding, yet many fail at a basic perceptual primitive: signed image-plane motion direction. On simple videos of a single object moving left, right, up, or down, most Video-LLMs perform near chance, with above-chance cases largely attributable to prediction biases rather than genuine direction understanding.
