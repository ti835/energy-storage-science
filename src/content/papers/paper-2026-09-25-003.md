## Abstract

Imaging an inhomogeneous medium with waves rests on the sensitivity of a boundary measurement to a change inside. Radar, seismology, medical ultrasound, and diffuse optics cast this sensitivity as a kernel of area-specific form. Here we show that all of them are built from two classes.
