## Abstract

We investigate how quadratic torsion modifies the equilibrium structure of white dwarfs in covariant $f(T)=T+ΞT^2$ gravity. Static spherical configurations are calculated with a fixed equation of state for cold carbon matter, including relativistic electron degeneracy and Coulomb lattice corrections.
