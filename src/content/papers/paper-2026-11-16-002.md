## Abstract

We study single-electron scattering in a tight-binding chain capacitively coupled to double quantum dots, which act as dynamical two-level-system impurities with electrically tunable level splittings. For a single impurity, we solve the scattering problem exactly and show Feshbach-like perfect elastic reflection, whose resonant condition distinguishes the initial two-level state.
