## Abstract

We resolve the admissibility problem for scattering transforms: every Parseval filter bank on $\mathbb{R}^d$ whose low-pass filter is nonvanishing at the origin induces a norm-preserving scattering transform, without requiring any analyticity, frequency-localization, or geometric covering assumptions.
