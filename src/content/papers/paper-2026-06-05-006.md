## Abstract

The unconventional superconductor 4Hb-TaS$_2$ is a natural heterostructure that can be broadly understood as interleaving Mott-like and metallic layers. We study the properties of this material as a function of quenched disorder in the form of Se/S substitution and find that while disordered samples show bulk superconductivity, clean samples do not.
