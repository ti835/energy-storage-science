## Abstract

The strongest constraints on the velocity-dependent ($p$-wave) annihilation of weakly interacting massive particle (WIMP) dark matter were derived from the deep potential wells of galaxy clusters. Even weaker signals can be extracted from sufficient aggregated clusters, by cross-correlating $γ$-rays with large-scale structure tracers or stacking over extensive cluster catalogs.
