## Abstract

We present MATChing HEFT Amplitudes (MATCHA), a Mathematica package designed for leading-order (LO) matching of an ultraviolet (UV) model to the Higgs Effective Field Theory (HEFT). MATCHA performs the matching of non-decoupling effects $\mathcal{O}(1)$ to the LO HEFT lagrangian for an arbitrary number of Higgs fields.
