## Abstract

We propose a random Euclidean CFT description of three-dimensional de Sitter gravity, with fixed central charge and primary spectrum but fluctuating operator product coefficients. Our construction starts from compact regions assembled from generalized hyperbolic tetrahedra. We analytically continue the gravitational amplitudes computed from these triangulations to de Sitter parameters and use them to infer OPE correlations.
