## Abstract

The Pointwise Dimension is a diagnostic that reveals the effective dimensionality of classical trajectories in a $2N$-dimensional phase space. The essence of this diagnostic is to find a scaling according to which density changes with radius $r$ around a point of interest in phase space. Particularly, regular trajectories are confined to an $N$-dimensional invariant torus, while chaotic trajectories explore a $(2N-1)$-dimensional energy surface.
