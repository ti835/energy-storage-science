## Abstract

Neutrino oscillations establish that neutrinos are massive, providing the only laboratory detection of physics beyond the Standard Model. Direct kinematic experiments bound the electron-neutrino mass to $m_{ν_e} &lt; 0.45$ eV (KATRIN, 90% CL), implying $\sum m_ν\lesssim 1.3$ eV.
