## Abstract

We study the radiative decay $Υ(1S)\to η_bγ$ and the Dalitz decay $Υ(1S)\to η_b e^+ e^-$ within the framework of the Covariant Confined Quark Model. We provide theoretical predictions for the hadronic form factor, the radiative decay constant, and the decay branching fractions.
