## Abstract

New flavour non-abelian gauge groups, which may arise as part of a fundamental theory of flavour, can lead to distinctive flavour-transfer processes. When restricted to the lepton sector, such processes partially mimic the standard charged current interactions at low energy. We explicitly study such constructions with various flavour structures, and investigate systematically all relevant accelerator-based constraints, exploring specific experimental signatures for these models.
