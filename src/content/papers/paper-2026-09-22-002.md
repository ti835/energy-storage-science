## Abstract

In a recent preprint (arXiv:2511.09556), we exhibited an inverse energy cascade for the 3D Navier-Stokes equations which results in "instantaneous" Type I blow-up and failure of uniqueness in certain sharp regularity classes. Our purpose here is to elucidate this phenomenon further in the setting of the Obukhov dyadic model, and to compare it to the better-known phenomenon of finite-time blow-up. When intermittency is low ($α\leq 2$), we recover our previous result from the Navier-Stokes setting
