## Abstract

We present the public software release of Parnassus, a Python/PyTorch, GPU-compatible framework for fast detector simulation and reconstruction in particle and nuclear physics. Parnassus provides a user-friendly framework with interchangeable detector models: neural models can emulate computationally expensive Geant4-based detector simulation and reconstruction chains, while parametric models provide PyTorch implementations of selected Delphes-style detector responses.
