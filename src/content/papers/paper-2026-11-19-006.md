## Abstract

Hybrid architectures have become mainstream among open-weight language models. Their global-attention and bounded-state layers maintain heterogeneous caches whose demands vary with context length and concurrency. We present TideCache, a serving system on CPU--GPU superchips with three components for allocating GPU memory among caches and managing their residency across GPU and host memory.
