## Abstract

As observed by Kienzle and the second author, rank-two $\mathcal{O}(3)$-twisted Higgs bundles on the projective line encode all hyperbolic crystals of genus two: the spectral curves are the unit cells, and the Hitchin fibers are the abelian Brillouin zones.
