## Abstract

Recently, two modes of spin wave excitations (magnons) were detected in Raman scattering spectra of MnTe2. It is an unconventional magnet that features a pyrite-type crystal structure hosting a non-collinear, non-coplanar antiferromagnetic (AFM) spin order below the Néel temperature.
