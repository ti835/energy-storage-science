## Abstract

We report on the results of our XMM-Newton and NuSTAR campaign targeting V1674 Her in October 2023. These observations were timed to investigate the post-nova quiescent state 28 months after the 2021 outburst, although the optical luminosity was still slowly declining. The X-ray spectra are well-modeled by a multi-temperature thermal plasma.
