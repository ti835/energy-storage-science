## Abstract

We present StringQFT as a textbook collection for string constructions of quantum field theory and as infrastructure for AI-assisted theoretical physics. The collection comprises 18 volumes, 190 chapters and 3,795 pages, covering brane probes, brane webs, geometric engineering, F-theory constructions, wrapped M5-branes and exact worldsheet constructions. Its open-book benchmark inventory contains 1,073 items, including 499 with automatic finite-answer checking. For retrieval-augmented generation
