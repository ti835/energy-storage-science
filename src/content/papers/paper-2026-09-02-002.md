## Abstract

We show that a single dark-photon dark matter framework can simultaneously account for two recently reported anomalies: the halo-like Galactic gamma-ray excess identified by Totani in 15 years of Fermi-LAT data, and the $E_{\rm nr}=248\pm23\,({\rm stat})\pm23\,({\rm sys})$ keV nuclear-recoil event of interest reported by the LUX-ZEPLIN Collaboration.
