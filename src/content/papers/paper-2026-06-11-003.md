## Abstract

Orbital-optimized (OO) density functional calculations provide a time-independent, variational route to electronic excitations that complements the presently widely used time-dependent density functional theory (TDDFT). As the orbitals are optimized in a state specific way, these methods can provide a balanced description of excited states with different character, thereby overcoming several limitations of practical implementations of TDDFT.
