## Abstract

We report a novel solution of three-dimensional Galilean Anti-de Sitter gravity. The theory can be formulated as a topological gauge theory invariant under the doubly extended Newton-Hooke group in three dimensions. The solution possesses four conserved charges: energy, angular momentum, spin and rest mass. Interestingly, the magnitude of the spin turns out to bounded from above by the rest mass.
