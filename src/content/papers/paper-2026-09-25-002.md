## Abstract

We present the implementation of a multidimensional general-relativistic Boltzmann solver for neutrino transport using the finite volume method. We extend the general-relativistic magnetohydrodynamics solver \texttt{Gmunu} to discretize the full $6$D phase space. We discretize the momentum space in spherical coordinates in the comoving frame, and the position space in the lab frame in Cartesian, cylindrical or spherical coordinates.
