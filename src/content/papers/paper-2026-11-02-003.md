## Abstract

Diffract-before-destroy schemes for atomic-scale electron mapping assume that ultrashort X-ray pulses outrun radiation damage. Although atomic cores move over tens of femtoseconds, outer electrons respond much faster, compromising their locations. We test this limit in elemental silicon using broadband, energy-chirped X-ray pulses to encode time with few-femtosecond resolution.
