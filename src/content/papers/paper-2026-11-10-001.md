## Abstract

We numerically solve the ground state wavefunction of SU(2) BFSS matrix quantum mechanics. We compare ground correlators against islands obtained using the matrix bootstrap and find agreement up to five significant figures. The power law tail of this wavefunction determines the three-point amplitude, which agrees with the computation of Herderschee and Maldacena.
