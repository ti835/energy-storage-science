## Abstract

Chiral spin liquids (CSLs) are paradigmatic fractionalized phases, yet simple exact parent Hamiltonians for them remain scarce. In this work, we construct exact spin parent Hamiltonians for partially magnetized CSLs realizing various bosonic topological orders. Our construction relies on an exact Hubbard-type parent Hamiltonian for bosonic lattice fractional quantum Hall (FQH) states, valid at arbitrary repulsive interaction strengths.
