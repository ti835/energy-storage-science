## Abstract

We present a control-variate method for reducing the variance of power spectrum covariance matrix estimates from simulations of large-scale structure. The key idea is to pair each mock simulation with a cheap Zeldovich-approximation realization sharing the same initial conditions, and to use the known statistical properties of the Zeldovich field to remove correlated sample variance from the covariance estimator.
