## Abstract

Consider level-$k$ $SL(2,R) \times SL(2,R)$ Chern-Simons theory on a semi-infinite solid cylinder, with a Wilson line in the unitary principal continuous series inserted along the time direction at the center of the spatial disc. We argue that at the $τ=0$ cut, this Euclidean path integral prepares the bulk dual of a heavy Virasoro primary above the BTZ threshold, with Drinfel'd-Sokolov (DS) reduction playing a crucial role in converting the affine module into a Virasoro module.
