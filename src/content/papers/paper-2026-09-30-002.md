## Abstract

Convex relaxation hierarchies provide lower bounds to the ground state energy of quantum many-body systems that can be computed in polynomial time on a classical computer, at any fixed hierarchy level. However, scaling these methods to large systems and accurate approximations remains challenging due to the computational cost of traditional solvers and the scarcity of efficiency guarantees.
