## Abstract

The antiferromagnetic biquadratic spin-1 chain is gapped, $SU(3)$ symmetric, and integrable. Standard arguments suggest that its low-energy physics is described by the $\mathbb{CP}^2$ nonlinear sigma model at $θ=π$. Using the Bethe ansatz, we show that its low-energy excitations can be interpreted as deconfined domain walls, just as in the sigma model.
