## Abstract

The LUX-ZEPLIN (LZ) experiment has reported an event consistent with a $248$ keV nuclear recoil. Explaining the absence of lower-energy recoil events typically calls for some form of upscattering that kinematically forbids such events. In this work, we present a framework in which the LZ observation has a neutrino origin, with atmospheric neutrinos providing the dominant flux in the required energy range.
