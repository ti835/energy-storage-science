## Abstract

Reconstructing the initial conditions of the Universe from late-time tracers would unlock cosmological information buried by non-linear structure formation and astrophysics. We reconstruct the initial density field at $z\sim300$ from simulated 21-cm and CO(1-0) line-intensity maps at $z\sim8$ generated with LIMFAST. Using a three-dimensional U-Net, we reconstruct the initial conditions and evaluate its impact on cosmological parameter constraints. The two tracers probe complementary environments
