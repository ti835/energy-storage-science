## Abstract

We propose a method of realizing non-Abelian braiding of fractionalized quasiholes in the Laughlin fractional quantum Hall phase at $ν=1/3$ with realistic two-body interactions within the lowest Landau level. It is numerically shown that low-lying gapped excitations near $ν=1/3$ are contained almost entirely within the null space of the three-body Moore-Read model Hamiltonian.
