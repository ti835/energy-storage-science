## Abstract

We study a new class of M2-brane matrix models associated with affine BC and twisted affine AD quivers. These models exhibit the characteristic $N^{3/2}$ scaling of the free energy in the large $N$ limit. We establish a Fermi gas formalism and demonstrate that their grand canonical sum is given by a Fredholm Pfaffian, which is in parallel with the affine D quiver matrix models.
