## Abstract

We prove that the planar Dacorogna--Marcellini energy $f_γ(A)=|A|^4-2γ|A|^2\det A$ is quasiconvex exactly when it is rank-one convex, i.e. if and only if $|γ|\leq\frac{2}{\sqrt3}$. The proof uses a monotonicity property of the energy functional along the componentwise heat flow. As a corollary of our method, we show that for homogeneous quartic polynomials on $2 \times 2$ matrices invariant by left and right rotation, quasiconvexity is equivalent to rank one convexity.
