## Abstract

Quantum coherence underlies collective quantum phenomena and emerging quantum technologies. Quantum gas microscopes have transformed quantum simulation by providing projective snapshots of many-body states with single-atom resolution, but spatially resolved measurements of off-diagonal correlations have remained elusive.
