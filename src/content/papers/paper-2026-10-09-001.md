## Abstract

We determine a universal sector of the spectrum of light operators in four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=1$ class $\mathcal{S}$ superconformal field theories arising from M5-branes wrapped on a compact hyperbolic Riemann surface $Σ_2$. We work at large $N$, analysing Kaluza--Klein fluctuations about the dual Bah--Beem--Bobev--Wecht anti-de Sitter backgrounds for general values of the twist parameter $z$.
