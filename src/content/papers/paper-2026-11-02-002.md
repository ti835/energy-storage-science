## Abstract

We present Mercury, a relativistic $N$-body code for cosmological simulations in the $α$-basis formulation of Horndeski gravity. Mercury extends a particle-mesh scheme by incorporating the dynamics of the additional scalar degree of freedom and the corresponding modified Einstein equations, expressed in terms of the time-dependent functions $α_{\rm K}, α_{\rm B}, α_{\rm M}, α_{\rm T}$ and the effective Planck mass $M_*^2$. This generalises our previous implementation of Horndeski subclass models
