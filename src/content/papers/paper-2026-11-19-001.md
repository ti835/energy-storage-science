## Abstract

Two regular black hole horizons need not have an entropy product fixed by the charges and couplings. In Einstein--Maxwell--scalar theory, we construct asymptotically flat branches with products above or below the squared extremal entropy. Regularity selects their horizon scalar values, and different branches can share the charges, couplings, and asymptotic scalar. Each product stays constant as mass and asymptotic scalar vary.
