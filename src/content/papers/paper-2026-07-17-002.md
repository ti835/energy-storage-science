## Abstract

Geometric scaling was conjectured and observed at the ISR more than 50 years ago. We argue that it still holds at the LHC. We show that the dip-bump structures of the differential elastic cross-sections exhibit a remarkable regularity, namely that the ratio of the bump to dip positions is constant from 23 GeV to 13 TeV.
