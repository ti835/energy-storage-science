## Abstract

Generating entanglement rapidly and reliably is essential for quantum information processing, communication, and metrology. Dissipative preparation is attractive because engineered reservoirs robustly drive a system toward an entangled target, yet relaxation can carry a substantial time cost. Here we formulate the entanglement Mpemba effect, whereby an initially less entangled state overtakes a more entangled state under the same open-system dynamics.
