## Abstract

We study the complete cosmological collider (CC) signatures from right-handed neutrino loops with chemical-potential enhancement. These CC signals are induced by the inflaton-right-handed-neutrino interaction and can be extracted in the collinear squeezed configuration, which allows the right-handed-neutrino triangle loop to be factorized into the hard and soft parts.
