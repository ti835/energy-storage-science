## Abstract

The boundary quantity that captures the growth of black-hole interiors quantified by holographic complexity remains unknown beyond 2d dilaton gravity. We provide a critical analysis of a partition-function construction of Krylov spread complexity for thermofield-double states that provides a dimension-independent boundary reconstruction from semiclassical holographic partition functions, while developing the present dynamical and bulk construction for the BTZ saddle.
