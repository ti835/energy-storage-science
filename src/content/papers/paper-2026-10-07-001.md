## Abstract

We show that the radial sector of a Carroll-Weyl gauged null string in Poincaré $\mathrm{AdS}_D$ is locally trivial in BRST cohomology. In a quotient-adapted BFV basis, the radial coordinate, its conjugate momentum, and the associated ghosts form a contractible quartet.
