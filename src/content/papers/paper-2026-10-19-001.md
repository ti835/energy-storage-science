## Abstract

We show that quantum systems on non-amenable lattices, whose boundaries constitute an extensive fraction of the system, host novel forms of bulk ordering with no Euclidean counterpart. Combining boundary-retaining iDMRG with an exact symmetry-fixing duality, we investigate $p&lt;5$ state clock models on the coordination-3 infinite Cayley tree. We find that between the ordered and disordered phases lies an emergent intermediate phase with bulk order induced by an arbitrarily weak boundary field r
