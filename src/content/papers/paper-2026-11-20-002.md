## Abstract

It is commonly stated in the literature that T violation is zero in neutrino disappearance channels. We show this claim is not general: it holds only for symmetric matter profiles or when the T/CP-violating phase ($δ$) is 0 or $π$, that is, when the Jarlskog invariant is zero.
