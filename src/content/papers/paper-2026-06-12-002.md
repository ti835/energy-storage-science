## Abstract

We introduce a basis for tree-level meromorphic scattering amplitudes suitable for describing pion scattering in the large-N limit. The basis is constructed as linear combinations of Lovelace-Shapiro-like amplitudes with varying Regge slopes and intercepts. The resulting amplitudes satisfy by construction the fundamental requirements of analyticity, crossing symmetry, and Regge behavior.
