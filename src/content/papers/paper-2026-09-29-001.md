## Abstract

Using the effective field theory of inflationary fluctuations extended by a massive isocurvature scalar, at unit sound speed, we compute the primordial bispectra generated by every cubic interaction of dimension four or less in the unitary gauge, with the curvature--isocurvature quadratic mixing resummed to all orders in the dimensionless mixing strength $λ=ρ/H$.
