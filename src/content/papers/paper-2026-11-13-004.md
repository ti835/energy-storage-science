## Abstract

We consider the Vlasov--Navier--Stokes system on $\mathbb T^d\times\mathbb R^d$, $d\ge2$. For smooth data and every $p&lt;2$, $q&lt;\infty$, we construct infinitely many weak solutions with velocity in $L^p_tL^\infty\cap L^1_tW^{1,q}$ and bounded, renormalized distribution function; infinitely many of them violate the energy inequality.
