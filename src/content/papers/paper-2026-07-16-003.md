## Abstract

We propose an experimentally motivated model in which the Mpemba effect can be controlled through the system's inertia. The model describes a polymer undergoing a denaturation transition whose force-extension curve contains a plateau that slows relaxation. When the system is initially prepared at a higher temperature or under a weaker stretching force, the bead accumulates greater kinetic energy, allowing it to cross the plateau more rapidly and thereby producing the Mpemba effect.
