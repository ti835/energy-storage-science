## Abstract

We solve large-$N$ two-dimensional QCD with fundamental quarks---the 't Hooft model---on rigid AdS$_2$ and dS$_2$. Their isometries preserve the null gauge $A_-=0$ and in affine coordinates the curvature enters the light-front Hamiltonian only through the special-conformal generator of the null line.
