## Abstract

We prove that, above a constant temperature threshold, Gibbs states of local noncommuting qudit Hamiltonians exhibit exponential decay of mutual information and conditional mutual information (CMI). Specifically, we prove exponential decay of the CMI for arbitrary spatial tripartitions $A \sqcup B \sqcup C$ which need not comprise the entire system, thereby establishing a \textit{uniform Markov property} for high-temperature Gibbs states.
