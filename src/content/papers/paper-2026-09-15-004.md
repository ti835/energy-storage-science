## Abstract

The area compressibility modulus is a key descriptor of lipid membrane mechanics, but the molecular interactions that determine this elasticity are not evident from the total modulus alone. Here, molecular dynamics simulations of a POPC bilayer described by the coarse-grained Martini 3 force field were combined with virial stress analysis to decompose the area compressibility modulus into contributions from molecular groups and their interactions.
