## Abstract

We study the three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equation on the cubic torus with a constant odd-viscosity operator given by the vertical derivative of the curl. Arbitrary real, mean-zero, divergence-free initial data and an independent steady force, both in the second-order Sobolev space, admit a unique global strong solution when the magnitude of one fixed odd-viscosity coefficient is at least a norm-uniform sufficient threshold.
