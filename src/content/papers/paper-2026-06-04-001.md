## Abstract

We construct data-driven solutions to the Hubble tension, in light of recent data from the Atacama Cosmology Telescope (ACT DR6) and the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI DR2). We search for the minimal modification to the recombination history through a time-varying electron mass $m_e(z)$ that increases the best-fit $H_0$ inferred from CMB data toward the SH0ES value, without worsening the fit to the data.
