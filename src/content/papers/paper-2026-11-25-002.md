## Abstract

We propose a broadband search for ultralight axion dark matter through the rotation of laser polarization induced by the axion--photon coupling. Two stratospheric balloons flying at altitudes of 30--40 km form a free-space optical link. One balloon sends a linearly polarized beam to the other, which reflects it back to a polarimeter on the first balloon.
