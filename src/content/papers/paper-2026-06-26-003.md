## Abstract

The 21-cm absorption line of neutral hydrogen has been a long hypothesized observational feature of the spectra of Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs). The difficulties associated with detector noise in extracting HI absorption have been previously studied. We test the role that scintillation plays in the HI absorption line's detectability, and characterize the regimes where a realistic FRB may yield the 21-cm line. We build an efficient model to simulate diffractive scintillation arising from FRB passage
