## Abstract

We analyse the large-time asymptotics of the Schrödinger time evolution of a free quantum particle on the Erdős-Rényi graph $\mathbb{G}(N,d/N)$, where the quantum Hamiltonian is given by the adjacency matrix of $\mathbb{G}$. Starting from a state localized at a vertex, the wave function is approximately radial, and we characterize precisely its asymptotic form.
